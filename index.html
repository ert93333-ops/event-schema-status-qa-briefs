FAQ

Event schema QA constraints are explicit.

Does it crawl event pages?

No. It produces a launch QA brief from pasted metadata and notes only.

Does it call Search Console or Rich Results Test?

No. This MVP is browser-local and does not call Search Console, Rich Results Test, ticketing platforms, or external APIs.

Does it connect to Eventbrite, Ticketmaster, or an event platform?

No. Ticketing status and offer details are pasted as notes for static QA only.

Does it provide ticketing or legal advice?

No. It is technical SEO and launch QA guidance, not ticketing, legal, compliance, eligibility, ranking, indexing, event operations, or attendee-support advice.
